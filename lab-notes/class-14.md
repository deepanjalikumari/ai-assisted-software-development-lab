# Class 14: Find Your User

I worked with Group 9 on an idea for IIT Gandhinagar campus residents who travel to off-campus destinations such as the airport, Kalupur Railway Station, or central Ahmedabad.

Today, residents often rely on friends or informal group chats to find people travelling at a similar time. They can miss potential co-passengers and end up paying the full cab fare alone. Our proposed web app would bring campus travel requests together and help match riders by destination and departure window.

I argued that the idea is worth exploring because it could make it easier to share rides and reduce the cost of travelling off campus. Before building it, we need to confirm that this is a frequent and important problem for residents, rather than assume that a matching app is useful. The target user should be a specific campus resident who makes these trips, not students in general. I also raised questions about whether a web app is the right form, what the first useful version should include, and how much we should build for the hackathon. Talking to residents will help us answer those questions and keep the scope grounded in their needs.

### First draft of testable statements

These are starting points for discussion with users and may change after interviews:

1. A resident can create a travel request by entering a destination and departure window.
2. A resident can view other open requests with the same destination and departure window.
3. A resident can join an open travel request and see that they have joined it.
4. The number of residents in a travel request cannot exceed the seat count entered by its creator.
5. A resident can cancel their own request, but cannot cancel a request created by someone else.
