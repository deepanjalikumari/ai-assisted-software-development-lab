# Class 15: Test Your Idea

## I

I worked with Group 9 on our idea for helping IIT Gandhinagar residents find co-passengers for trips to places such as the airport, Kalupur Railway Station, and central Ahmedabad.

The question our group could not answer was **“Who, exactly?”** We have described a possible user as a campus resident who travels off campus, but we have not identified a specific person by name and role or spoken with them. Without that conversation, we also do not know the details of their last trip, how often the problem happens, what it costs them, what tools they already use, or whose permission or data we would need. I should ask about their actual travel process before deciding that our app is the right solution.

### Statement I wrote

**A resident can create a travel request by selecting a destination and departure window, and the request appears in the list of open requests.**

**Test:** Create a request with a destination and departure window, then open the list of requests and check that the new request appears with those values.
